# SB Launcher Privacy Policy (template)

## What we store

- Roblox OAuth tokens (encrypted at rest on the API)
- SB session tokens
- Optional favorites, launch history, theme presets, outfit presets

## What we do not store

- Roblox passwords
- `.ROBLOSECURITY` cookies
- Payment card data

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared with Roblox only as needed to complete OAuth and API calls you initiate.
