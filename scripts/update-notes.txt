SB Launcher 2.4.0

- Discover: new Paid Places section for Robux paid-access experiences.
- Owned paid places show Play; if you don’t own them, the card shows the Robux price.
- Price opens the Roblox game page to buy; the rest of the card opens the launcher game page.
- Ownership uses your inventory (Open Cloud), not playability — so owned games are detected correctly after sign-in.
- Requires inventory OAuth scope (Inventory badge in Settings). Re-sign in if it’s missing.
