SB Launcher 3.5.1 — Profiles, rooms & visual polish

- Favorite games on profiles can be arranged horizontally, vertically, in a grid, or freely by dragging. Tall layouts no longer leave a blank banner.
- Room chat, player cards and moderation controls have been improved.
- Home features richer event cards; private server setup is simpler.
- Liquid Glass surfaces can use adjustable blur, and the animated symbol background runs more smoothly.
- Startup and update overlays have been refined.
